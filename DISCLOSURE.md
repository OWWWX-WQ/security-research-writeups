# Responsible Research and Disclosure Policy

## Scope of Published Work

The material in this portfolio comes from authorized CTF environments, isolated laboratories, self-owned systems, or explicitly approved security assessments.

## Public Redaction

Public reports omit live target identifiers, credentials, tokens, signing material, customer data, exact payment identifiers, private infrastructure details, and operational exploit payloads. Public examples use generalized names and synthetic objects.

## Verification

Original authorization records, raw evidence, timestamps, and test-object ownership records are retained offline. They may be shared privately with legitimate reviewers when the applicable confidentiality terms permit it.

## Accuracy

Findings are labeled as confirmed, code-confirmed, conditional, not established, or retracted. This portfolio does not claim CVE assignments, vendor acknowledgements, employment, or production impact that did not occur.

## Reporting Issues

This repository is not a disclosure channel for vulnerabilities in unrelated third-party systems. Questions about the published methodology or portfolio content may be raised through the repository's GitHub issue tracker without including sensitive information.
